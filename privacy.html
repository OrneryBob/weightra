Weightra Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Applies to Weightra for iPhone and Apple Watch

The short version. Weightra is a workout and body tracker built by one independent developer. Everything you log stays on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and, with iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud account. Weightra has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party ever receives your data. The developer cannot see your data.

1. Who is responsible

Weightra is developed and published by Dylan Sola, an independent developer based in the United States. For anything in this policy, email dylan.sola@icloud.com or use "Contact the Developer" inside the app.

2. Information Weightra stores

All of the following is stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud database (see section 4). None of it is sent to the developer.

  • Workouts you log — exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, distances, routines, tags, notes and effort ratings.
  • Body data you enter — body weight, tape measurements, weight goals, sleep you log by hand, and your body profile (biological sex, date of birth, height, body type).
  • Progress photos you take — kept inside Weightra's own storage, never added to your photo library.
  • Game progress — XP, levels, stats, quests, challenges, skill-tree progress and streaks derived from the above.
  • Settings — units, rest timer, reminders, effects and similar preferences.

3. Apple Health

Weightra works best connected to Apple Health, but connecting is optional and you can skip it during setup. You choose exactly which categories to allow, and you can change or revoke any of them at any time in the iOS Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices → Weightra.

What Weightra reads, and why

  • Workouts, including those recorded on Apple Watch — to import them into your log and award XP.
  • Heart rate, active energy and resting energy — to show live workout metrics and summarize imported workouts.
  • Steps, flights climbed, walking/running distance and cycling distance — for daily quests, activity stats and XP.
  • Activity ring summary — for daily activity stats.
  • Sleep — for sleep quests and recovery stats.
  • VO₂ max — for the cardio fitness rating and its history.
  • Body weight, height and waist circumference — to keep your body trends and BMI current.
  • Date of birth and biological sex — to pre-fill your body profile and pick age- and sex-matched norms.
  • Workout effort scores — to show the effort you rated on Apple Watch workouts.

What Weightra writes

  • Workouts you record with Weightra on Apple Watch (with their heart rate, energy, distance and step samples) and the effort score you give them, so they count toward your Activity rings and appear in the Fitness app.
  • Body weight, height and waist measurements you log in Weightra.
  • Sleep you log by hand in Weightra.

How Health data is handled

Health data is used only to provide the features above. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never used for any data mining unrelated to the app's own features, never sold, and never disclosed to any third party. Weightra does not store Health data on any server. If iCloud sync is on, values Weightra has imported from Apple Health (such as weight readings and imported workouts) are included in the app data kept in your private iCloud database, which is described next; you can turn sync off at any time to keep everything on the device only.

4. iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default so your data follows you between your iPhone and other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Weightra uses Apple's CloudKit to store your data in the private database that belongs to your Apple Account. That database is protected by Apple and is not accessible to the developer or anyone else. You can turn sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync; your data then stays on the device only. "Erase All Data" in Settings removes your data from the device and from your iCloud database. Apple's handling of iCloud is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

5. Apple Watch

The Weightra watch app records live workouts using Apple Health on the watch and mirrors the session, your XP and rest timers to your iPhone over Apple's local device-to-device connection. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

6. Camera and progress photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a progress photo. Photos stay inside Weightra's own storage on your device (and in your private iCloud database when sync is on). They are never uploaded to the developer or to any third party, and they are not added to your photo library unless you export them yourself.

7. Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders (rest timer, quests, weigh-ins, streaks and similar) are scheduled locally on your device, and you can turn each one off in Settings → Notifications. Weightra does not send marketing notifications and does not run a notification server; the only background notification channel is Apple's silent iCloud sync signal. Widgets and Live Activities show data that is already on your device.

8. Purchases

Weightra Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions; Weightra never sees your payment details, and Apple does not share your name or Apple Account information with the developer. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction. Your purchase is verified on your device using Apple's StoreKit, and nothing about it is sent to any server of the developer's.

9. What Weightra does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No servers of the developer's own; no data leaves your Apple ecosystem.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. Weightra does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No data sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

10. Contacting the developer

"Contact the Developer" in Settings opens a message in your own Mail app addressed to the developer. The message includes an optional, visible diagnostics block (app version, device model, iOS version and sync status) that you can delete before sending. Anything you send is used only to answer you and fix problems, is kept only as long as needed to do that, and is never shared.

11. Your choices and your rights

  • Export — Settings → Your Data → Export gives you a copy of everything Weightra has stored.
  • Delete — Settings → Your Data → Erase All Data removes everything from the device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.
  • Health access — change or revoke any category in the iOS Settings app under Health.
  • Sync — turn iCloud sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync.
  • Notifications — turn any reminder off in Settings → Notifications, or in the iOS Settings app.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is no personal information held by the developer to access, correct, port or erase; you hold all of it yourself through the controls above. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another region with privacy rights and you have a question or request anyway, email dylan.sola@icloud.com and it will be answered within 30 days. Weightra does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

12. Data retention and security

Your data stays on your device until you delete it, and in your iCloud database until you erase it, turn sync off and remove it, or delete it from your Apple Account. On-device data is protected by iOS device encryption; iCloud data is protected by Apple's iCloud encryption, including end-to-end encryption if you have Advanced Data Protection turned on.

13. Children

Weightra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because Weightra collects no information for the developer at all, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side; a parent can remove a child's data by deleting the app or using Erase All Data.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this address with a new effective date. Changes that materially affect how your data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
